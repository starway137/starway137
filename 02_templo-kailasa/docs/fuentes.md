- [KL-04] Kramrisch, S. (1946). *The Hindu Temple*. Calcutta: University of Calcutta.
- [KL-05] Hardy, A. (1995). *Indian Temple Architecture: Form and Transformation*. New Delhi: IGNCA / Abhinav.
- [KL-06] Michell, G. (1977). *The Hindu Temple: An Introduction to Its Meaning and Forms*. London: Paul Elek.
